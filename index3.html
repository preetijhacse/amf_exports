Good to Know

Common questions before you reach out.

Where is everything manufactured?

Every piece is manufactured in our own workshop facility in Noida / New Delhi, India by master Karigars with over 30 years of hand-forging experience.

Can you produce to a custom design requirement?

Yes — all our manufacturing is made to order (MTO). We routinely develop prototypes and custom tooling based on brand reference sketches or specifications.

Who do you typically partner with?

We partner with premier luxury retail brands, hotel chains, and high-end tabletop distributors worldwide — over 150 international brand partners to date.
